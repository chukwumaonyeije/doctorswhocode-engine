So I study the pipes now. Who pays. Who coordinates. Where the friction sits. This YouTube transcript is not about medicine, but it maps the playbook we keep losing to.

- The MAGA influencer ecosystem operates like a professional marketing operation with a lobbying problem: group chats for synchronized messaging, party circuits for access, and pay-to-post platforms that route money through consulting firms to avoid disclosure.
- Because US disclosure rules focus on goods and traditional ads, political “calls to action” and narrative pushes can be paid without being labeled as ads.

## Physician-developer takeaway

This is not a “people behaving badly” story. It is an incentives-and-pipes story. Medicine keeps getting wrecked by the same architecture.

## What is structurally broken:
- Disclosure asymmetry: FTC and FEC regimes require “ad” labels for products, but political messaging and “call your rep” pushes often dodge ad disclosure. Health narratives can hide inside those lanes.
- Pass-through opacity: Consulting firms act as black boxes. Money goes in clean, comes out coordinated, and nobody has to label a post.
- Platform engagement economics: The algorithm pays on virality, not veracity. There is no native provenance for health claims.
- Workflow gaps on our side: Health systems have no real-time narrative intelligence, no rapid-response copy that is consistent and signed, and no metrics that treat coordinated disinformation like an outbreak.

## What changes when physicians build:
- We design disclosure as a protocol, not a plea. Build a public, machine-readable “influence ledger” schema that any publisher or influencer can attach to posts. If money or material support touched a health or policy message, the JSON says who, when, and how much.
- We ship provenance into clinical comms. Sign official guidance with NPI-bound keys. Let patients and platforms verify that a message came from a real clinician or institution.
- We operate “narrative SRE.” A small cross-functional on-call that monitors coordinated spikes in harmful health narratives with graph analytics, then ships counter-messaging within hours, not weeks.
- We demand symmetrical rules. Professional societies should push regulators to require ad-like disclosure for paid political influence that affects health behaviors. If you get paid to push a petition about vaccines or public health policy, label it like an ad.

## Build list for the next 90 days:
- Open-source a minimal Influence Disclosure Schema v0.1 (JSON-LD) with fields for sponsor, amount, in-kind support, targeting, and script provenance.
- Prototype a provenance signer for health org posts using DKIM-like signatures tied to verified domains and NPIs.
- Stand up a lightweight coordination detector: ingest public posts, cluster identical phrasing, time-synchronize spikes, and flag likely campaigns touching a health topic.
- Publish a transparency report when your hospital or society runs paid outreach. If we expect it of others, we do it first.
